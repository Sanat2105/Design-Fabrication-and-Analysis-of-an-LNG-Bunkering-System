# # Design, Fabrication and Analysis of an LNG Bunkering System

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the **design, fabrication, and analysis of a Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) bunkering system** for the safe and efficient transfer of LNG from a storage facility to a marine vessel.

The project emphasizes **mechanical design, cryogenic compatibility, structural integrity, safety, and fabrication considerations**.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Design an LNG bunkering system for marine applications
- Develop 3D CAD models and fabrication drawings
- Select suitable materials for cryogenic service
- Perform structural analysis of critical components
- Prepare the Bill of Materials (BOM)
- Consider safety and operational requirements

## ⚙️ Major Components

- LNG Storage Tank
- Double-Walled Insulated Piping
- LNG Transfer Lines
- Cryogenic Valves
- Pumps
- Flanges and Fittings
- Bunkering Hoses
- Pressure & Temperature Instruments
- Safety / Emergency Shutdown System
- Tank Support Structure

## 🔬 Analysis

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) was performed on critical components to evaluate their structural performance under operating conditions.

### Parameters Evaluated

- Von Mises Stress
- Total Deformation
- Applied Pressure
- Structural Constraints
- Factor of Safety
- Load Distribution

The analysis results were used to identify critical regions and improve the design before fabrication.

## 🏭 Fabrication Process

1. Preparation of engineering drawings
2. Material selection and procurement
3. Component cutting and preparation
4. Welding and joining
5. Assembly of piping and mechanical components
6. Installation of valves and fittings
7. Dimensional inspection
8. Pressure and leak testing
